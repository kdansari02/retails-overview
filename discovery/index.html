Foodbridge Module Retails — Discovery Prototype

Can a retailer open a QR/link straight into Shop — no login wall — search and add items with qty steppers, check the wholesaler's details, review and place an order, then track it and manage their profile? Same features on desktop, laid out for a larger screen.

Addendum 002 · Mobile-first shop flow

Shop · Orders · Profile — one app, two layouts

One real "app" per device (no separate pages per tab — Shop/Orders/Profile switch in place). Company Info, Review Order and Order Detail are overlays on top of whichever screen you're on: a bottom sheet on mobile, a modal / side panel on desktop.
